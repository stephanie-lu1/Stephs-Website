TEDxCMU

Speaker Curation Team (2023-24), Head of Speaker Curation (2024-25), Co-President (2025+)

In TEDxCMU, we host an annual main event where we invite speakers from all around the world to speak on a topic they are passionate about. As co-president, I oversee the 6 teams that comprise our organization (speaker curation, logistics, public relations, innovation, design, salon) and work with the speakers that are coming to our campus.

In the past, I have worked with a social media influencer disability advocate, a CMU professor, and a student speaker in structuring their ideas into talks and getting them ready to perform on stage. It truly is a rewarding process to be able to work with candidates, as some of them come in with one potential idea, and we help to flesh it out.

CMU Run Club

Prime Minister (2024-25), Co-President (2025-26), Mentor (2026-27)

I LOVE RUNNING!! It is one of the things I am most passionate about. I began running during my freshman year of college, and instantly loved being apart of CMU Run Club's community. I ran my first half marathon at the Pittsburgh Marathon that spring with an 11 minute mile and tbh that was probably one of my most proudest moments. I have ran in other Pittsburgh races since, including the famous Great Race 10K and the EQT 10 miler. There are also smaller community events, like the Donut Dash (run 1 mile, eat 6 donuts, run another mile) and local 5Ks that I love. Also I'm D1 stravafluencer and I genuinely love Strava so much. It started out as a way to build fitness... but I fear it has gotten competitive.

In run club, we organize and host community running events that are open to CMU students/staff/faculty. Every year, Pittsburgh has 3 main races (Great Race, EQT 10 Miler, Pittsburgh Marathon) that our club provides reimbursements for.
